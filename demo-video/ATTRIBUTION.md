# Asset attribution

- **Voiceover:** supplied master recording `assets/polaris-voiceover.mp3` (ElevenLabs, generated by the team). Used unaltered.
- **Footage:** all imagery is captured from the Polaris Window application (`captures/`), rendered from NASA LRO LOLA terrain (LDEM_80S_80M) and NASA/JPL NAIF SPICE ephemeris. Validation figures and values (r = 0.990 sunlight, r = 0.992 Earth visibility) come from the app's comparison with NASA PGDA maps (Mazarico et al. 2011). A grayscale grade is applied for the monochrome identity; no data is altered.
- **Sound design:** `assets/ambient.wav`, original, synthesized procedurally (sine drones and filtered noise). No samples or music.
- **Typeface:** Inter / Inter Display (SIL Open Font License), installed locally.
- **Software:** Remotion, React, Playwright, FFmpeg.

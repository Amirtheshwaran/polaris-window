    A weather forecast for Moon landings

    Near the Moon's south pole the Sun barely rises, and mountains can hide the Earth. Polaris Window uses real NASA terrain and orbit data to show when a landing site gets sunlight (for power) and a clear view of Earth (for radio contact).

    1. Pick a location on the 3D map, or open Locations at the top right.
    2. Read its forecast: sunlight and Earth view over the next year.
    3. Find a Landing Date to rank every landing window from 2025 to 2030.

    Use the timeline at the bottom to move through time and watch real shadows sweep across the craters.
